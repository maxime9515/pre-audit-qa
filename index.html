Why pre-audit QA

Clean coverage cuts the audit bill — and the calendar.

Auditors price the gaps they have to reason about. Close the core fund-flow coverage first, and their scope shrinks before the clock starts.

01

Auditors require core fund-flow coverage

Deposits, withdrawals, swaps, transfers, fee paths — the money-moving routes are exactly what an audit is bought to review. If they aren't covered, that work lands on the audit invoice.

02

Clean coverage can typically trim the fee by ~15–25%

When core fund flows already have passing, readable tests, the audit scope narrows and the bill follows. This is a common industry estimate, not a guarantee — but pre-audit QA is the cheapest line item you will ever add to that budget.

03

The tests ordinary suites skip

Real-wallet flows driven through the live UI, and money-math boundary cases — rounding, precision, dust, overflow edges. That is where DeFi bugs actually live, and where generic suites stop.
